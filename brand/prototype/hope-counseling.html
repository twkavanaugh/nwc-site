Who we are

A church-based counseling center.

In 2001, North Wake Church saw a need in Wake Forest and the surrounding communities to address depression, anxiety, and marriage problems from a distinctively biblical counseling perspective.

We are a team of committed Christian counselors and members of North Wake Church who volunteer to provide free or donation-based biblical counseling to those in need, under the oversight of our church Elders and Pastoral Staff.

Please know

We are not licensed counselors, psychiatrists, or psychologists. Many of our counselors are formally trained, with a Master's in Biblical Counseling or Divinity from an accredited seminary, or are pursuing one.

What to expect

Three things you can count on.

  1. 01Proverbs 18:13

    A listening counselor

    In every session — especially the first several — your counselor will work hard to listen and understand your situation.

  2. 02Isaiah 9:6

    A look at Scripture

    We place our hope not in a counselor or program, but in the Wonderful Counselor. The source of our counseling is Scripture.

  3. 032 Timothy 3:16–17

    Real work

    Counseling is not a passive activity. Your counselor will give you homework to complete before your next session.

Services

What we help with.

We counsel across the emotional and relational spectrum.

Emotional
  • Depression
  • Anxiety
  • Grief
  • Trauma
  • Anger and other emotional struggles
Relational
  • Pre-marital
  • Marital
  • Parenting
  • Conflict resolution
Group
  • Living Waters — group counseling focused on sexual brokenness, with separate men's and women's small groups
Outside our scope
  • Substance abuse cases requiring detox
  • Counseling of minors younger than sixteen
  • Emotional struggles with psychotic features
How to begin

The first step toward change.

We believe the first step toward change is to admit you have a problem — to yourself, to God, and to others who care and are able to help.

  1. 01

    Request counseling

    Complete the counseling request form. Your request is treated with care and confidentiality.

  2. 02

    Hear from our Scheduler

    Our Scheduler will typically contact you within 24 hours to discuss the best way to serve you.

  3. 03

    Begin

    Bring a pen, a journal or notepad, and a Bible if you have one. A $35 refundable deposit is due before your first session.
