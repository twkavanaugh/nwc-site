Our heart

Tangible help. Lasting hope.

For physical needs

These funds, collected from North Wake Church, are intended to tangibly show recipients God's great love for every individual through assistance from the local church.

Beyond physical needs

Beyond caring for physical needs, our great desire is that everyone would hear of their profound need for Jesus Christ while receiving prayer from team members.

How to apply

Three steps, in person.

Funds are released quarterly.

  1. 01

    Apply in person

    Please complete an application in person at our church office reception desk in Building Five. The application takes 10 minutes to complete.

  2. 02

    Meet and pray

    The care team schedules a meeting to pray for applicants. The interview typically takes 45 minutes with our care team.

  3. 03

    Funds dispersed

    Funds are released quarterly, and dispersed on a first-come, first-served basis until they are depleted for each quarter.

Please note

Applicants who received funding from North Wake in the last 12 months must wait to reapply until that 12-month period has passed.
