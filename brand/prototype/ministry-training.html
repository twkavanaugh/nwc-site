Our approach

From general to specific.

Training followers of Jesus to minister to others is one of the greatest privileges and responsibilities that God has given the church.

Our approach is to move from general training in personal ministry to specific training in areas of vocational ministry.

Personal Ministry Pathway

Length
9 months
For
All followers of Jesus
Credit
Available for SEBTS students

Personal ministry is that to which all followers of Jesus have been called into, making disciples for Jesus. Often we are not sure where to start when it come to ministering to others, so over a 9 months period, we try to cover some of the basics. Including;

  1. 01Who is God at the most essential level?
  2. 02How do I shape my life to more closely follow Jesus daily?
  3. 03What is the gospel?
  4. 04How do I engage my neighbors with the gospel?
  5. 05How does the church engage in discipleship?

We seek to address these concerns through a mixture of guided readings, study of Scripture, mentorship, project work, and intentional writing. This internship style approach engages the mind, heart, and hands of those asking how they might become disciple makers for Jesus.

Elder Preparation Pathway

Length
9-month cohort
For
Men seeking ordained ministry
Toward
Church planting, pastoral ministry, or other ordained assignments

This pathway offers specific equipping for those seeking to serve the church through church planting, pastoral ministry, or other ministry assignments requiring ordination. This 9 month elder trainee cohort seeks to prepare, assess, and deploy men to advance Christ’s mission throughout underserved contexts of the United States.

Prepare
Assess
Deploy

These objectives are addressed through a deeply relational process of considering the role elders occupy and the character necessary to fill it. Through reading, discussion, observation, practice, and mentoring, elder trainees embark on the slow journey to eldership.

As the term pathway implies, aspiring to eldership takes time and guidance. It is our goal to help men start their journey with sobriety and patience.

03
Pathway

Missionary Sending Pathway

For
Men, women, and families
Process
Assessment, coaching, and support
To
The ends of the earth

Sending men, women, and families to the ends of the earth to advance God’s global mission has always been the passion of North Wake Church. We continue in this rich heritage of sending through leveraging decades of experience to send those who desire to go. Assessment, coaching, and support permeate our deployment process.

We have learned that the best way to send well is to ensure that missionary candidates:

  1. 01
    Walk closely with Jesus
  2. 02
    Love those close to them sacrificially
  3. 03
    Passionately speak of Christ when they have the opportunity

All of these values rest on being known, which requires time, transparency, and training.

We are happy to send our best, to the best of our ability, for the good of the world and glory of Christ.
