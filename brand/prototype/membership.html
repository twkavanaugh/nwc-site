The membership process

Five steps, walked together.

It begins simply — by letting the church office know you’d like to pursue or consider membership.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Contact the church office

    Simply contact the church office to let us know you’d like to pursue or consider membership.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Get connected

    Our office will let you know the dates for the next New Members Course and encourage you to:

    • a.
      Get connected to a Grow Group

      We’ll send you a Grow Groups brochure to help you find one.

    • b.
      Begin attending an Adult Discipleship Course

      We’ll send you information on current courses.

    • c.
      Complete “My Story”

      We’ll send you a short online form to share your story with us.

  3. 3

    Step 3: A get-to-know-each-other conversation

    We’ll then schedule a brief, 30-minute conversation with a staff leader — a chance to get to know each other.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Attend the New Members Course

    Attend the course and complete the following:

    • AAttend at least 4 of the 6 classes
    • BTurn in all required documents
    • CSign the membership covenantRequired
    Missed a class?

    If fewer than 4 classes are attended, you’ll attend the missing classes at the next course to be considered for membership.

    New Members Course details ↗ (opens in a new tab)
  5. 5

    Step 5: Presented and affirmed

    New members are presented to and affirmed by the congregation.
