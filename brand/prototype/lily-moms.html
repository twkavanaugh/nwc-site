Our heartbeat

Deep joy. Real exhaustion.

Motherhood with little ones can bring both deep joy and real exhaustion.

We believe no mom should walk through these little years alone and we strive to provide a safe place where you can come as you are, find encouragement, and connect with other women who understand this unique season of life.

Regular rhythms

What a LILY morning looks like.

We meet on the 1st and 3rd Wednesdays from October through May, 9:00–11:30 a.m. at North Wake Church.

  1. 01

    Breakfast

    (yes—uninterrupted!)

  2. 02

    A talk

    From an experienced speaker on topics related to motherhood.

  3. 03

    Meaningful conversation

    We discuss motherhood, faith, everyday life, and everything in between.

  4. 04

    Creative activities

    And opportunities to connect with other moms.

Beyond the meetings

There are also monthly playdates outside of our bi-monthly meetings.

For your little ones

While moms gather, children enjoy safe and engaging childcare where they can learn, play, and build friendships of their own.
