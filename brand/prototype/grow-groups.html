The heartbeat

Shaped not just in what we know, but in what we love.

At the heart of this ministry is a longing to see God form people who love Him deeply, care for one another genuinely, and live with a growing concern for their neighbors.

We believe spiritual growth is not meant to happen in isolation, but through shared life by opening God’s Word together, praying honestly, bearing burdens, celebrating victories, and encouraging one another toward Christlikeness.

Our hope is that, over time, people are shaped not just in what they know, but in what they love — developing real affection for God, resilient faith in everyday life, and a readiness to live on mission wherever God has placed them.

Regular rhythms

Life together, week by week.

Grow Groups typically meet weekly in homes across different days of the week.

  1. 01Weekly

    Open God’s Word

    Reflect on and apply the passage from that week’s sermon together.

  2. 02Weekly

    Pray honestly

    Pray together, bear burdens, and celebrate victories side by side.

  3. 03Through the year

    Serve and care

    Shared service opportunities, one another discipleship, and practical care for one another.

  4. 04Always

    Share the hope

    Groups regularly pray for boldness and opportunity to share the hope of the gospel with others.

What participation looks like

Showing up consistently, being known and cared for, and learning to follow Jesus together in the ordinary rhythms of everyday life.
