What to expect

The flow of a Sunday.

Same rhythm every week, no surprises. Whether it's your first time or your hundredth, here's what your morning looks like.

  1. Photo · Arrive
    01

    Arrive

    Pull into our main lot off S Main St — greeters in orange vests will wave you in. Plenty of free parking, with reserved spots up front for first-time guests.

  2. Photo · Welcome
    02

    Welcome

    Stop by the Welcome Wall in the lobby. Grab a coffee, get a campus map, and meet someone who can answer questions or walk you to Kids check-in.

  3. Photo · Worship
    03

    Worship

    Service runs about 75 minutes — songs (modern and traditional), Scripture reading, prayer, and a sermon. Communion is offered the first Sunday of each month.

  4. Photo · Stay
    04

    Stay

    We host a 'Next Step' lunch after the 10:45 AM service on the second Sunday of every month. It's the easiest way to ask the staff anything you want.

Common questions

FAQ.

What should I wear?+

Whatever you'd wear to dinner with friends. Most people land somewhere between jeans-and-a-shirt and business casual.

What about my kids?+

We have safe, joyful programming for birth through 5th grade in both services. Check-in opens 30 minutes early — we'll walk you there.

Is there parking?+

Yes — free, with reserved guest spots near the main entrance.

Will I be singled out?+

Nope. We don't ask first-time guests to stand up, raise hands, or wear anything that marks them out.

How long is the service?+

About 75 minutes, including a sermon of around 35 minutes.

Can I just watch online first?+

Absolutely. Every Sunday is livestreamed and archived — link below.
