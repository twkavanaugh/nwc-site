Why we exist

Know. Glorify. Grow. Go.

North Wake Women’s Ministry exists to help women know the love of God, to develop a passion to glorify God with every part of their lives, to grow into the likeness of Christ, and to go in the power of the Holy Spirit to reach women for the kingdom of God.
