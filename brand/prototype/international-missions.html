We eagerly hope and long for the day in heaven when we will be praising King Jesus seated on the throne alongside peoples from every nation, tribe, and language.
Sending as discipleship

Going in love, near and far.

Therefore, our mission statement at North Wake Church reminds us to focus daily on going in love to our neighbors, both near and far. For decades, we have deeply desired to send numerous church members to spread the kingdom of Jesus Christ to every corner of the earth.

And once sent, the entire church remains committed to caring for those brothers and sisters who have gone to share God's love for all.

Our model

This process of sending members is a part of our church discipleship model where we intentionally equip them for overseas service.

Through our church ministries

Showing God's heart for the nations.

We seek to show God's heart for the nations through our church ministries.

  • Pray
    Every Sunday

    In our Sunday gatherings, we pray for the nations and for those we have sent, whom we call our Far Flung Family.

  • Give
    ~40 families

    We also sacrificially give a portion of our tithes and offerings to partially support roughly 40 families sent out from our church.

  • Focus
    3 Sundays

    We annually set aside three consecutive Sundays for our missions conference Entermission.

  • Equip
    Break Bread

    For those that are feeling led by the Father to serve overseas, we seek to equip them by having them join our Break Bread Program.

Annual missions conference

Entermission.

Entermission is the opportunity for our church to intently focus on God's work around the world through our local engagement, church plants around the USA, and throughout the world.

The Break Bread Program.

For those that are feeling led by the Father to serve overseas, we seek to equip them by having them join our Break Bread Program.
