Mission · Church planting

Church planting is at the very center of who North Wake is and will always be.

This is largely due to two realities.

Our heritage

First, we as a church family were planted in Wake Forest in the early 90’s by another older and more established church. Our very history and heritage depends on the courage of Providence Church (Raleigh NC) to take the risk of starting a new fellowship in an area that needed one.

02

Our conviction

Second, we believe this is the burden and responsibility of vibrant churches; to expand Christ’s kingdom into place where God is working.

Heritage + conviction

So based on our heritage and conviction, we likewise have planted several churches over the years. Some continue to this very day, others have run their course and passed the baton to others to continue the work of making disciples.

The churches

Churches we’ve planted and partner with.

Church Planting Partners

Those whom we continue to partner with to encourage their daily flourishing.

Some of these works we have planted directly out of our fellowship, others have been works that partner with existing churches to assist in the church planting process.

Kingdom work

We believe that church planting is kingdom work, that collaborates with healthy planters, churches, and organizations to advance Christ’s mission to reach all people everywhere.

How we engage

We continue to engage in church planting through celebrating our self sustaining churches, supporting our church planting partners, and developing new church planting endeavors. We do this through diligent prayer, generous investment, and on going development of the next generation of church planters.
