Who we are

A Gospel-centered free health care clinic.

North Wake Church provides leadership and space for the clinic. It is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, primarily staffed by volunteers, and 100% funded by donations.

What we offer
  • Comprehensive primary care
  • Limited dental care
  • Health education
  • Case management
Who we serve

Our neighbors in the North Wake region, including Franklin, Granville, and Vance counties.

Adults 18 years and older whose household income is below 200% of the federal poverty level.

Outside our scope of practice
  • Pain management and prescription of controlled substances
  • Obstetrical care
  • Pediatric care
  • Emergency care
  • Complex psychiatric care
  • Surgical care
  • Reconstructive dental work
  • Dentures
