Baptism Sunday

A morning of public professions of faith — in both services.

Questions? Email the church office — pending verification

Baptism is one of the most joyful moments in the life of our church — a believer's public declaration that they belong to Jesus. We'll celebrate baptisms during both morning services, and the whole church family is invited to witness and rejoice together.

If you're considering baptism, or want to understand what it means and why we practice it, we'd love to talk with you before the day arrives.
