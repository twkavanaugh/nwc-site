As I write this meditation, I do not yet know what Bible passage will be at the heart of Sunday’s sermon. But I do know that when we gather in Jesus’s name we are called to behold him, hear from him, and adore him as a church.

That’s what the songs are meant to help us with this week. So to prepare your heart for Sunday, I invite you to take some time to listen to the songs we will be singing together. If you know them, sing along! If you don’t, get acquainted with them so that we can praise Jesus together with one voice on Sunday!

You can find the Spotify playlist [here](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/6OvOoCr92gw5jnoTTItXBc?si=eda26f256b964dd9).

> *“Kings of the earth and all peoples, princes and all rulers of the earth!  Young men and maidens together, old men and children!*

> *Let them praise the name of the Lord,  for his name alone is exalted;  his majesty is above earth and heaven.”*

> Psalm 148:11–13
