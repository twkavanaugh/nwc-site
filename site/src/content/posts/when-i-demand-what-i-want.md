Anger rarely announces itself honestly. James 4 traces it back to the desires that
war within us — the demands we make of God, of others, and of our circumstances —
and points to a better way: humbling ourselves before God, who gives grace to the
humble. This article, drawn from the HOPE Counseling Center training material, looks
at where anger comes from, what James 4 says about it, and the path from demand to
humility.
