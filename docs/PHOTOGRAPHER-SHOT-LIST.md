# Photographer Shot List — North Wake Church Website (v1, 2026-07-03)

Working document for the website photography session. Compiled from the live site build + a responsive pilot test of the photo-hero pattern. Questions: Todd.

## Global technical requirements (apply to every website shot)
- **Landscape orientation, minimum 2000px wide** delivered (wider is better; we crop, we don't upscale).
- **Key subject in the upper two-thirds of frame** — the site darkens the bottom of hero images for text contrast; anything low in frame gets swallowed.
- **Center-weight the subject horizontally.** On phones the sides of the image crop away hard — only roughly the middle third survives. If the subject matter is at the edges, mobile viewers never see it.
- **Avoid large bright/blown-out areas dead center** (windows, glare, white walls) — headline text sits there over a dark overlay; bright backgrounds fight it.
- Candid > posed throughout. Warm, natural light preferred.
